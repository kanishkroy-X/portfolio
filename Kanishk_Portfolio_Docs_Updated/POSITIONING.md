# POSITIONING

## Directions considered (rejected/superseded)
- Growth PM / APM
- AI Product Engineer / Full-Stack AI Builder
- AI Operations & Creative Technologist
- Hybrid Product Builder — AI Systems & Growth Strategy

“Growth Strategist” was dropped because it overstates commercial experience.

## Final positioning
**Headline:** “I build digital products & AI-powered systems.”

**Subline:** “Product thinking × AI × hands-on execution.”

**Personal narrative:**
> I take messy ideas, break them down, and turn them into working products, systems, and creative experiences.

**Underlying pattern:**
> I don’t just generate an output. I build a system around it.

This is not:
> “I know React, Python, AI, Figma, Premiere, etc.”

This is:
> “I take messy problems and turn them into working systems.”

## Proof architecture
| Project | What it proves |
|---|---|
| RandomChat | Product/system execution |
| AI Ad Production Studio | Creative + AI production execution |
| AI Research Analyst | Complex AI/system thinking |
| Wellbeing Nutrition CRO | Product/growth thinking |
| CleanPDF | Utility/product engineering |

RandomChat’s specific pattern:
**product idea → architecture → state management → safety → localization**

AI Ad Production’s specific pattern:
**brief → references → prompts → generation → selection → editing → sound → QA**

AI Research Analyst’s specific pattern:
**research → evidence → verification**

Wellbeing Nutrition’s specific pattern:
**research → friction → hypothesis → experiment → prototype**

CleanPDF’s specific pattern:
**utility problem → privacy-conscious architecture → working product**

## Identity phrases
- **IDEAS → SYSTEMS → REAL THINGS**
- **DECONSTRUCT → BUILD → TEST → REFINE**

## Guardrails
- Never claim elite/expert status without evidence.
- Never invent metrics such as MAU, latency, retention, revenue, or conversion lift.
- Never present a conceptual workflow as a measured production result.
- Every factual capability claim should trace back to a real, checkable artifact: repo, video, screenshot, diagram, test, or document.

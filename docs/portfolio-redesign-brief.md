## 1. Core Objective

The strategic objective of the redesign is to position Kanishk Roy as an **early-career multidisciplinary builder operating a personal Product + AI + Creative Technology Studio**.

The website must emphatically reject common portfolio tropes:
- ❌ **Not a generic developer portfolio:** No endless logo walls of 30 programming language badges, no toy calculator apps, no standard todo lists.
- ❌ **Not a student resume website:** No GPA listings, coursework lists, or academic detachment.
- ❌ **Not a template portfolio:** No generic Webflow or Framer template look with identical centered cards.
- ❌ **Not a generic AI agency:** No purple/cyan neon glow gradients, no robotic cyborg heads, no floating 3D spheres, no "revolutionizing the future with AI" marketing claptrap.
- ❌ **Not a SaaS landing page:** No pushy pricing tables, fake user logos, or aggressive sales funnels.
- ❌ **Not an animation spectacle:** No gratuitous scroll-jacking, bouncy physics, cursor-following blobs, or distracting parallax.
- ❌ **Not a collection of random projects:** No disjointed experiments that fail to tell a coherent personal story.

Instead, the website communicates that Kanishk is someone who:
- **Builds real digital products** with resilient architecture and clean code.
- **Thinks deeply about users, ergonomics, and friction**.
- **Uses AI as a precision production tool** rather than a gimmick.
- **Understands business realities, economics, and conversion funnels**.
- **Creates high-end visual and creative work** with cinematographic taste.
- **Experiments rapidly and fearlessly**.
- **Takes an ambiguous idea from concept → system architecture → shipped result**.

## 7. Project Presentation Philosophy

Projects must never be presented as superficial cards with a thumbnail and a list of programming languages. Every project is an **investigation and an execution story**.

### Mandatory Narrative Arc
```
┌───────────┐     ┌───────────┐     ┌───────────┐     ┌───────────┐     ┌───────────┐
│  CONTEXT  │ ──► │  PROBLEM  │ ──► │ DECISIONS │ ──► │ EXECUTION │ ──► │  OUTCOME  │
└───────────┘     └───────────┘     └───────────┘     └───────────┘     └───────────┘
```
1. **Context:** What prompted this project? (Market gap, client brief, technical friction).
2. **Problem:** What breaks when people attempt this conventionally?
3. **Decisions:** Why did Kanishk make specific, counter-intuitive technical or design choices? (e.g., Why Durable Objects instead of Redis? Why 2K storyboards before entering prompt generation?).
4. **Execution:** Real code, real diagrams, real frame pacing, real verifier rules.
5. **Outcome & Evidence:** Shipped URL, recorded master, test suite, or measurable usability gain.
6. **Learnings:** What broke during development and what was learned?


### Visual Characteristics
- **Tonal Identity:** Restrained, mature, intelligent, tactile, quietly confident.
- **Surface Depth:** Warm architectural off-white (`#F7F7F5`) with `texture-paper.svg` in light mode; deep matte graphite (`#111111`) with `texture-grain-dark.svg` in dark mode.
- **Accents:** Reserved electric blue (`#3157FF` light / `#6B82FF` dark) used only for interactive states, key numbers, and active badges. Never used as giant muddy background gradients.
- **Hairlines:** Crisp 1px borders (`#DCDCD8` / `#30302D`) framing components like technical blueprints.
- **Motion Philosophy:** Micro-motion only. Hover transitions capped at 150ms–250ms with `cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)`. No spinning icons, no gratuitous parallax.

## 18. Core Content Principles

Every sentence on the redesigned portfolio must obey seven strict editorial rules:

1. **Specific > Generic:** Write *"In-memory room isolation coordinated by Cloudflare Durable Objects"* instead of *"Fast, scalable cloud architecture"*.
2. **Evidence > Claims:** Show the 2K resolution storyboard and playable 1080p commercial master instead of claiming *"Expert in video generation"*.
3. **Outcomes > Features:** Explain why ephemeral sessions eliminate user churn instead of simply saying *"No login required"*.
4. **Thinking > Tech Lists:** Articulate the architectural trade-off of why a certain tool was chosen over alternatives.
5. **Clear Language > Buzzwords:** Completely ban terms like *"synergy"*, *"disruptive"*, *"rockstar"*, and *"cutting-edge"*.
6. **Personal Voice > Corporate Facade:** Speak in the first person (`"I built"`, `"I designed"`, `"I decided"`). Avoid referring to a single-person studio as an anonymous corporate *"We"*.
7. **Show > Tell:** Let the interface interact, let the video play, let the diagrams be inspected.

## 21. What Must NOT Change (Non-Negotiables)

During any future redesign phase, the following elements are **strictly protected**:

1. **Astro SSG Foundation:** The static architecture must remain. Do not replace Astro with a heavy client-side Single Page Application (SPA) or bloated full-stack framework.
2. **The Two-Pillar Balance:** RandomChat and AI Ad Production must remain equally prominent flagships. Never hide either one or demote them to secondary links.
3. **Real Deliverables & Proof:** Never replace real YouTube video embeds, real 2K storyboards, or real Cloudflare architecture schematics with conceptual mockups or stock imagery.
4. **Anti-Slop Restraint:** Strict ban on purple AI gradients, spinning 3D shapes, cursor particles, and generic developer buzzwords.
5. **Zero-FOUC Theme Engine:** The 3-mode theme switcher (Auto/Light/Dark) with its inline blocking script must remain intact.
6. **Factual Integrity:** Every capability claim must trace back to a verifiable artifact in the repository or live web.

## 22. What Should Change (Prioritized Roadmap)

### Critical Improvements
- **Problem:** Heavy, uncompressed hero raster images (`hero-editorial.jpg` at 866KB, `hero-architecture.jpg` at 762KB).  
  **Why It Matters:** Drags down mobile First Contentful Paint (FCP) and Largest Contentful Paint (LCP).  
  **Direction:** Convert all assets to modern `.webp` and `.avif` formats (< 90KB) with responsive `srcset`.
- **Problem:** External Google Fonts request pulls 4 families across 18 weights.  
  **Why It Matters:** Render-blocking network dependency on third-party servers.  
  **Direction:** Self-host the 4 core weights of `Geist` and `Geist Mono` locally as `.woff2` files in `public/fonts/`.

### High Priority Improvements
- **Problem:** YouTube iframes load ~500KB of external scripts on page initialization.  
  **Why It Matters:** Slows down initial interactive readiness and triggers third-party tracking.  
  **Direction:** Implement a click-to-load video facade that only instantiates the player upon user interaction.
- **Problem:** RandomChat live link (`https://randomcaht.online`) is hidden inside the case study.  
  **Why It Matters:** Visitors scanning the homepage miss the proof that the platform is live and operational.  
  **Direction:** Add a direct `Open Live App ↗` pill alongside the case study link on the homepage preview card.

### Medium Priority Improvements
- **Problem:** Low-contrast metadata tokens (`#666664` on `#111111`) fail WCAG AA ratios.  
  **Why It Matters:** Impairs readability for users with vision constraints.  
  **Direction:** Calibrate dark metadata tokens to `#8E8E93` or higher.
- **Problem:** Missing JSON-LD structured data.  
  **Why It Matters:** Search engines fail to associate Kanishk Roy as a recognized Person and Creative Work author.  
  **Direction:** Embed schema.org markup in `BaseLayout.astro`.

### Optional Improvements
- **Problem:** Contact is purely email-based (`mailto:` & Gmail compose).  
  **Why It Matters:** Some visitors prefer a frictionless 3-field in-page form.  
  **Direction:** Add a lightweight form on `/contact` backed by a serverless function.

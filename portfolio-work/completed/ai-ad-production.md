## One-Line Description
A disciplined 13-stage commercial production framework turning generative models into broadcast-grade video campaigns.

## Problem
Raw AI video generators produce visual novelty but fail at commercial narrative discipline. Without rigorous prompt architecture and camera control, output suffers from anatomical warping, style drift, temporal flicker, and lack of brand intent.

## What I Built
An end-to-end commercial production pipeline spanning strategic brand deconstruction, 2K storyboard framing, decoupled prompt architecture, multi-seed generative curation, and frame-accurate timeline editing with Foley sound design in Premiere Pro. Shipped two completed commercial spots: Adidas Samba (9:16 vertical) and Kreo Tech (16:9 widescreen).

## My Role
Creative Director, Prompt Architect & Post-Production Editor

## Tools / Technologies
Midjourney v6, Runway Gen-3, Kling AI, Adobe Premiere Pro, CapCut, Foley Mix, LUT Color Grading

## Key Features
- 2K resolution storyboard framing locking camera focal lengths prior to generative passes
- Decoupled prompt token architecture separating camera dynamics from material textures
- Multi-take generative curation gate keeping only the top 5% artifact-free footage
- Macro leather texture retention in Adidas Samba vertical reel
- Macro mechanical hardware cinematography in Kreo Tech widescreen film
- Custom Foley audio design and timeline editorial sync
